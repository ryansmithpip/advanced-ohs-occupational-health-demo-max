Our Commitment

How we protect your information

Advanced Occupational Health Services ("Advanced OHS," "we," "us") is committed to protecting the privacy and security of your protected health information (PHI). This notice describes how we may use and disclose your health information and the rights you have regarding that information, consistent with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) and applicable Washington State law.

We use your health information to provide and coordinate your care, to bill for services, and to support the operation of our practice — for example, coordinating with your employer, insurer, or Washington L&I on a workers' compensation claim. We will not disclose your information for other purposes without your authorization, except as permitted or required by law.

Important: forms on this website are not for emergencies and should never include sensitive medical details.

Your Rights

Your rights regarding your health information

You have the right to inspect and obtain a copy of your health records. Request access through our patient portal or contact us in writing.
If you believe your record is inaccurate or incomplete, you may request an amendment. We will respond in accordance with applicable law.
You may request that we communicate with you in a certain way or at a certain location — for example, by mail rather than phone.
You may ask us to limit what we use or disclose. We are not required to agree to all requests, but we will consider each one carefully.
Reply STOP to any message to opt out of SMS communications, or HELP for assistance. Standard message and data rates may apply.
Security Practices

Safeguards we maintain

Technical safeguards

Encrypted transmission (SSL/TLS), secure patient portals, access controls, and regular security reviews.

Workforce training

Staff are trained on privacy and security responsibilities, with role-based access to sensitive systems.

Minimum necessary

We limit use and disclosure of PHI to the minimum needed for the purpose at hand.

Business associate oversight

Vendors who handle PHI on our behalf are bound by HIPAA business associate agreements.
